# Contact page refresh

Matches the supplied contact screenshot. Retains existing phone numbers, support email, office addresses and hours, shared header/footer and chat widgets.

## Imagery

Built-in imagegen output converted to WebP:

- `public/contact/support.webp`: Photorealistic Nigerian support adviser in emerald blouse and headset at a laptop, right-aligned in a bright mint office, pale left-side negative space. No text or logos.
- `public/contact/cities.webp`: Three equal photographic panels of Lagos waterfront, Abuja city architecture and Ibadan skyline. Illustrative city imagery, not actual office photographs.

## Form and map

Original form simulated delivery. New form validates name, email, optional phone and message, then offers a mailto draft including the topic and all entered fields. UI clearly states nothing has been sent from the demo. Edit retains values. Message length capped at 500.

Office cards select a city-level OpenStreetMap view. Directions links use the existing street addresses with Google Maps. Map markers identify city areas rather than verified entrances.
