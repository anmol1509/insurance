# Travel and business landing imagery

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool and encoded as WebP.

- `public/travel/coast.webp`: Photorealistic Nigerian woman in a straw hat and backpack overlooking Santorini and a turquoise Mediterranean bay, small distant passenger plane, bright open left side, no text or logos.
- `public/travel/explore.webp`: Photorealistic traveller raising her arms above a mountain lake, right-aligned subject, shaded green negative space on the left, no text or logos.
- `public/business/building.webp`: Photorealistic modern glass and bronze office building in a Lagos-inspired landscaped business district, warm sunlight, right-aligned building and open sky on the left, no text or logos.

Reference claims and prices are not treated as verified product facts. The business builder uses the existing calculator for its four supported covers; monthly values are labelled annual-price equivalents, not payment offers. Other business cover enquiries go to contact. Travel cards are preference bundles, not invented insurer plans. Validated query parameters carry supported landing selections into the existing quote flow.
